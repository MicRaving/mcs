Harmonization

        
Every harmonized study ranked by the poolability-weighted missingness of the 11 essential
          slots. Click a study or a slot chip to open the assignment workspace at that gap and label
          the variable with value labels, example rows and codebook context. Machine statuses are
          filled / missing / ambiguous; n/a and confirmed come from coder assignments.
